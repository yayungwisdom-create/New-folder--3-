About Bishop of Souls Ministry

Committed to evangelism, prayer, discipleship, worship, missions and the spiritual development of believers.

Who we are

Called to bring souls to the Shepherd

Bishop of Souls Ministry is organised into departments and ministries that each serve a specific area of service, administration and spiritual development.

Through those departments, our weekly programmes, annual missions and two major conferences, we seek to equip people to grow in Christ and carry the Gospel to their communities and beyond.

“For ye were as sheep going astray; but are now returned unto the Shepherd and Bishop of your souls.”1 Peter 2:25 (KJV)
Leadership

Meet our leaders

The leaders God has appointed to shepherd this ministry and its Joel Network branch.

Dr. Johnson Okeniyi

Founder, Bishop of Souls Ministry

Johnson holds a PhD in Business and Management from Abertay University (Dundee, Scotland), an MSc in Project Management from Northumbria University (Newcastle, England) and a BSc in Management from Central University (Accra, Ghana). He is an Associate Fellow of the Higher Education Academy in the United Kingdom, an expert in strategic alliances between SMEs and larger organisations, a lecturer and an entrepreneur, passionate about raising entrepreneurs among young thinkers.

His Christian foundation was shaped by serving under, or being raised, trained or instructed by, the Christ Apostolic Church, Church of Pentecost, Mountain of Fire and Miracles Ministries, Redeemed Christian Church of God, Salvation Army, Methodist Church, Assemblies of God and the Baptist Church. A passionate counsellor, called by God as a child, his life is a living advertisement of the glory, power and mercy of Almighty God.

PhD · AbertayMSc · NorthumbriaBSc · Central UniversityAFHEA (UK)

Joshua Makafui Cudjoe

National Director, Joel Network Ministry

Joshua provides leadership and coordination for Joel Network’s school evangelism and campus ministry activities. He also serves as an Assistant to Dr. Johnson Okeniyi, supporting ministry leadership and related administrative and operational responsibilities.

Professionally, he is an IT Consultant and the CEO of IT Consult, and an Assistant IT Manager at Charis Ghana Limited. In ministry he serves as Youth Pastor at the Bishop of Souls Ministry headquarters in Ghana, contributing to youth discipleship, spiritual development, evangelism and leadership formation.

He is committed to using technology, leadership, mentorship and the preaching of the Gospel to advance God’s work and empower the next generation.
