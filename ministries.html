Our Ministries

Departments and ministries that each serve a specific area of service, administration and spiritual development.

Major branch

Joel Network Ministry

“Simply Fulfilling Prophecies.”

A major branch of Bishop of Souls Ministry dedicated to reaching, nurturing and discipling students through school evangelism, Christian mentorship, spiritual development and campus ministry, with its own leaders, coordinators, school outreach teams and campus fellowships.

Departments

Nine departments, one mission

Every department exists to help people encounter Christ and grow in Him.

🙏

Prayer Department

Coordinates prayer meetings, intercession, fasting programmes and other prayer activities.

📋

Administrative Department

Oversees ministry administration, records, correspondence, scheduling and general coordination.

💰

Financial Department

Manages the ministry’s finances, budgeting, financial records, expenditure and accountability.

📣

Evangelism Department

Coordinates evangelism outreaches, soul-winning activities, community evangelism and mission trips.

🎵

Music Department

Oversees worship, choir activities, musical rehearsals and music ministration.

🎥

Media Department

Manages photography, videography, livestreaming, audio-visual production, publicity and digital communications.

🎭

Drama Ministry

Uses drama, theatre and creative presentations to communicate biblical truths and the Gospel.

💃

Dance Ministry

Leads dance ministration and choreographed presentations as expressions of worship.

💻

Technology Department

Provides technical support, manages digital tools and systems, and supports the ministry’s technological operations.

Want to serve in a department?

Tell us about your gifts and which department you are interested in, and a leader will respond.
