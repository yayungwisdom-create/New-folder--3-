Privacy Policy

How Bishop of Souls Ministry collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Bishop of Souls Ministry (including its branch, Joel Network Ministry) is based in Accra, Ghana. Contact: bishopsouls@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

Contact form: your name, email address, optional phone number, type of enquiry and message. We use it only to respond to you.

Joel Network participation form: your name, email, optional phone, whether you are a pupil, student or graduate, age group, optional school and fellowship, an optional note, and your consent. We use it to review your request and follow up. Submitting the form does not automatically make you a member.

We do not ask for payment card details anywhere on this website.

Children and young people

If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before submitting the form. The form asks you to confirm this. We keep information about young people to the minimum needed.

Who can see your information

Submissions are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). Only authorised ministry staff who are signed in to the administration area can read them. They are not published on the website.

How long we keep it

We keep submissions only as long as needed to respond and to run the ministry’s programmes. You can ask us at any time to correct or delete your information by emailing or messaging us.

Cookies and third parties

The website does not use advertising or analytics tracking. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and connects to Google Firebase to display and save content. Videos are loaded from YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) only when you choose to play them. Your browser may store a small note locally to prevent repeat form submissions within a minute.

Your rights

We aim to handle personal data in line with Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). You may ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by contacting us.

Photographs

Photos are published with the ministry’s permission. To request removal of a photo of you or your child, contact us and we will act promptly.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
